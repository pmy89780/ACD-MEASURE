👁️ ACD Measure

Automatic horizontal distance between the pupil centre and the centre of corneal curvature, from temporal side-view eye images.

Capture or upload an unmarked temporal-view eye image to begin.

Points are detected automatically. If needed, drag the green (pupil) or red (corneal curvature) marker to fine-tune; the measurement updates instantly.
